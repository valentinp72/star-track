Solar filter check

Pointed at the Sun without a filter, the telescope blinds at once and its own parts can burn. Solar mode turns off the 15° keep-out zone around the Sun. Before turning it on:

  • a certified solar filter (ISO 12312-2) covers the whole front of the tube, fixed so that it cannot fall off;
  • the finder is capped or removed;
  • nobody looks through the eyepiece while the mount moves.

Solar mode turns off again at park and at power-on.

E-stop: how to get the mount going again

  1. 1Release the E-stop (on the mount: turn the knob).
  2. 2Re-arm the mount: the firmware waits for arm before it moves again.
  3. 3Find the position again: centre a known object and sync on it. With the drivers off the motors had no holding torque, so the mount no longer trusts its position (pos_ok false). In the simulator, "Centre" moves the tube onto the selected object as you would with the joystick.
