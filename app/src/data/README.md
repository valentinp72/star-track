# Catalogue data

## catalog.json

`catalog.json` is generated by [`scripts/build-catalog.mjs`](../../scripts/build-catalog.mjs)
from:

- the [HYG star database](https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg) 4.4
  (`data/hyg/CURRENT/hyg_v44.csv.gz`): stars to magnitude 5.0, proper motion
  applied to 2026.0;
- [OpenNGC](https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC) (`database_files/NGC.csv` and
  `addendum.csv`): Messier objects, and NGC/IC objects of magnitude 10 or brighter.

Both are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/),
and so is `catalog.json`. RA in hours and Dec in degrees, J2000 equinox.

Each object also has the title of its English Wikipedia article, when there is
one: the script asks Wikipedia (redirects followed, disambiguation pages left
out) for "Messier 42", "NGC 869", "IC 434", and for stars their Bayer name in
full ("Alpha Persei"), their Flamsteed name ("61 Cygni"), "Name (star)", then
the name.

To regenerate it, download the three files, then in `app/`:

```sh
node scripts/build-catalog.mjs hyg_v44.csv.gz NGC.csv addendum.csv
```

It needs internet for the Wikipedia titles, and takes a few minutes: Wikipedia
limits the request rate.

## pictures.json and ../../public/img/

Made by [`scripts/build-pictures.mjs`](../../scripts/build-pictures.mjs) for about
60 objects (the Sun, Moon and planets, showpiece deep-sky objects, two double
stars): the main image of each object's Wikipedia article, or a chosen
Commons file, kept only when it is on Wikimedia Commons under a free licence
(public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA), resized to 400 px and saved as WebP; and
the first three sentences of the article (CC BY-SA 4.0). `pictures.json` holds,
for each picture, its author, licence and Commons page, which the app shows
under it.

```sh
node scripts/build-pictures.mjs
```

It needs internet and ImageMagick (`convert`, `identify`).
